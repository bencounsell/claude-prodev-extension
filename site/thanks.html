Thanks for buying Hairline Pro

Your licence key is in your receipt email from Creem (check your spam folder if it hasn't arrived after a few minutes). To unlock Pro:

  1. Copy the licence key from the receipt.
  2. Open Hairline and click the gear icon to open Settings. Or right-click the Hairline icon in your toolbar and choose Options.
  3. Paste the key into Activate Pro and click Activate. Every tool unlocks straight away.

You can use your licence in up to 3 browsers. To move it, choose Deactivate this browser in Settings, then activate it in the new one.
