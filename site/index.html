How it works

Docked in the side panel, beside your page

Docked beside the page

Click the toolbar icon and Hairline opens in Chrome's side panel. Nothing covers the page, and your tools stay put as you switch tabs.

Knows your breakpoint

The panel narrows the page, so it shows the live viewport and breakpoint, like 900 × 800 · MD. Need full width? Switch to floating panels.

One keystroke away

Press Alt Shift K for the command palette, or jump straight to the inspector with Alt Shift I. Esc closes any tool.

CSS Inspector

Hover to read the CSS, click to change it

Hover to see the box model, typography and colors. Click to lock an element and edit any value right in the panel; the page updates as you type, and one click resets everything.

  • Margin, border and padding drawn on the page
  • Copy CSS or a selector in one click
  • Edits stay on your screen and reset on reload

FAQ

Common questions

Which browsers does Hairline work in?

Chrome, Microsoft Edge and Brave, version 116 or later. They all support Chrome's side panel and Manifest V3.

Does it work on every website?

It works on any normal web page. Browsers don't let extensions run on their own internal pages, such as settings, the new tab page or the extension store, so Hairline tells you when you're on one of those.

Do my edits change the real website?

No. Edits, deletions and moved elements only change what you see in your own browser, and they reset when you reload the page. Use Copy CSS or Export Element to take your changes with you.

What's the difference between the side panel and floating mode?

The side panel docks beside the page and keeps everything uncovered. Floating mode shows tools over the page instead, which keeps the full page width, handy when you need to check a desktop breakpoint. Switch any time in Settings.

How does the Pro licence work?

Buy once and you get a licence key. Paste it into Hairline's Settings to unlock Pro. It keeps working offline, and you can deactivate a browser and move the key whenever you like.

Does Hairline collect any data?

No. There's no analytics or tracking, and page content never leaves your browser. The only network requests are licence checks for Pro and Google Fonts when you preview a font. Read the privacy policy.

How does Send to AI work?

Pick an element or the whole page and choose a task, like recreating it in React + Tailwind or reviewing its accessibility. Hairline writes a detailed prompt with the markup, styles and colors, then opens Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini with the prompt ready to send (or on your clipboard). You use your own AI account, and Hairline never sends anything itself.
