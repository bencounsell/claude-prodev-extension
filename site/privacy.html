Privacy policy

Hairline is made by [SELLER: your name or company], [COUNTRY]. It is a browser extension that runs on your device. It has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't sell or share data with anyone.

What stays on your device

  • Page content you inspect, edit, export or capture is processed in your browser and never sent to us.
  • Your settings (theme, panel location, recently used tools) are stored with Chrome's storage, which may sync between your own browsers if you use browser sync.
  • Screenshots and images you save go to your Downloads folder.
  • Send to AI builds its prompt in your browser. Hairline never sends it anywhere: it opens your AI app (Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini) with the prompt filled in or copied to your clipboard, and nothing is shared until you send it yourself under that app's own privacy policy.

The only network requests Hairline makes

  • Licence checks. When you activate a Pro licence, and about once a day after that, Hairline sends your licence key and an instance name to our licence server, which checks it with our payment provider (Creem) to confirm it's valid. Nothing about the pages you visit is included, and we don't log keys.
  • Font previews. If you preview a font with Fonts Changer that isn't installed on your computer, the page loads that font from Google Fonts, as any website using Google Fonts would.

Permissions

  • activeTab and scripting: to show tools on the tab you open Hairline on.
  • storage: to remember your settings and licence.
  • downloads: to save screenshots and images.
  • sidePanel: to dock Hairline beside the page.
  • Optional "all sites" access: only if you allow it, so the side panel keeps working as you switch tabs. You can remove it any time in your browser's extension settings.

Purchases

Pro purchases are handled by Creem, our merchant of record, which processes your payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive your order and licence information, not your card details.
